# Publications

## Weakly Supervised Pneumonia Localization from Chest X-Rays

**Kiran Shahi, Anup Bagale**<br>
*Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Autonomous Intelligence*, 2025

A weakly supervised approach to pneumonia localisation using deep neural networks and Grad-CAM explanations, focused on clinically meaningful visual evidence without expensive pixel-level annotations.

[Read on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00456) · [View source](https://github.com/kiranshahi/pneumonia-analysis) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.54364/JAIAI.2024.1126)

## Background Replacement in Video Conferencing

**Kiran Shahi, Yongmin Li**<br>
*International Journal of Network Dynamics and Intelligence*, 2023

This work investigates real-time human segmentation and background replacement using convolutional and recurrent architectures, with attention to temporal consistency, visual quality, and efficient local inference.

[Read the paper](https://media.sciltp.com/articles/sciltp/257/pdf/6597b4c418b50.pdf) · [View source](https://github.com/kiranshahi/Real-time-Background-replacement-in-Video-Conferencing) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.53941/ijndi.2023.100004)
